HOW TO USE THE SPREADSHEETS IN GOOGLE SHEETS
--------------------------------------------

The .xlsx files open in Excel and Apple Numbers as-is. If you'd rather
work in Google Sheets, it takes about thirty seconds:

  1. Go to sheets.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click the folder icon near the top right (it says "Open file
     picker" when you hover over it).
  3. Choose the UPLOAD tab.
  4. Drag the .xlsx file in, or click "Browse" and find it.
  5. It opens as a Google Sheet. That's it — the dropdowns and the
     formulas all come across and keep working.

To keep your own copy separate from the original, go to
File > Make a copy once it's open, and rename it after your project.

On your phone: open the Google Sheets app, tap the + button, then
Choose template > no — tap the folder icon instead and pick the file
from your Files or Drive. Uploading from a computer first is easier.

Two notes: Google Sheets sometimes shows the dropdown arrow only once
you click into the cell — click the cell and look for the small
triangle on its right edge. And Sheets may lighten the dark styling on
import; the formulas and dropdowns still work exactly the same.
